# Key numbers — только явно указанные цифры из abstract (Europe PMC, 2015–2026)

Каждая строка: точная цитата из abstract + DOI. Цифры, которых нет в abstract, НЕ добавлялись. Пусто значит пусто.

---

## Объём силовой тренировки (гипертрофия)

- **Ralston 2017, J Sports Sciences** (мета-регрессия, 34 группы, 15 исследований):
  - «Each additional set was associated with an increase in effect size (ES) of 0.023 corresponding to an increase in the percentage gain by 0.37%.»
  - «the ES difference between higher and lower volumes was 0.241, which equated to a percentage gain difference of 3.9%»
  - DOI: `10.1080/02640414.2016.1210197`
- **Lopez 2022, Appl Physiol Nutr Metab** (volume-load-эквивалентные нагрузки):
  - «A pooled analysis of the standardized mean difference for 1RM strength outcomes across the studies showed a benefit favoring HL vs. LL and vs. ML and favoring ML vs. LL.»
  - «A pooled analysis of the standardized mean difference for hypertrophy outcomes across all studies showed no differences between training loads.»
  - DOI: `10.1139/apnm-2021-0515`
- **Borriello 2023, Sports Medicine** (пожилые/средний возраст, ≥50 лет):
  - «Multiple sets per exercise produced a greater effect than single sets on lower-limb strength (standardized mean difference [SMD] = 0.29; 95% confidence interval [CI] 0.07–0.51; mean difference [MD] = 1.91 kg; 95% CI 0.50–3.33) and muscle quality (SMD = 0.40; 95% CI 0.05–0.75) gains.»
  - «There were no differences between single versus multiple sets per exercise for upper-limb strength (SMD = 0.13; 95% CI −0.14 to 0.40…)»
  - DOI: `10.1007/s40279-022-01769-x`
- **Schoenfeld 2019, Med Sci Sports Exerc** (1/3/5 сетов, 8 нед):
  - «significant increases favoring the higher-volume conditions were seen for the elbow flexors, mid-thigh, and lateral thigh»; сила — «no significant between-group differences»
  - DOI: `10.1249/mss.0000000000001764`

## Proximity to failure / RIR

- **Robinson 2023, Sports Medicine** (SR+MA, 15 исследований):
  - «there was a trivial advantage for resistance training performed to set failure versus non-failure for muscle hypertrophy… [effect size=0.19 (95% confidence interval 0.00, 0.37), p=0.045]»
  - «no advantage for either resistance training performed to momentary muscular failure versus non-failure for muscle hypertrophy [effect size=0.12 (95% confidence interval −0.13, 0.37), p=0.343]»
  - «resistance training performed to high (>25%) versus moderate (20–25%) velocity loss thresholds [effect size=0.08 (95% confidence interval −0.16, 0.32), p=0.529]»
  - DOI: `10.1007/s40279-022-01784-y`
- **Robinson 2024, Sports Medicine** (дозозависимость RIR):
  - «Strength gains were similar across a wide range of RIR, while muscle hypertrophy improves as sets are terminated closer to failure.» (конкретные коэффициенты регрессии в abstract не приведены)
  - DOI: `10.1007/s40279-024-02069-2`
- **Refalo 2023, Sports Med Open** (острое утомление, 6 сетов жима 75% 1RM):
  - «Decreases in lifting velocity at 4 min post-exercise were greater for FAIL (−25%) versus 1-RIR (−13%) and 3-RIR (−8%)»
  - DOI: `10.1186/s40798-023-00554-y`
- **Zourdos 2021, J Strength Cond Res** (точность RIR-based RPE, сет 70% 1RM):
  - «RIRDIFF at 9RPE = 2.05 ± 1.73; RIRDIFF at 7RPE = 3.65 ± 2.46; and RIRDIFF at 5RPE = 5.15 ± 2.92 repetitions»
  - DOI: `10.1519/jsc.0000000000002995`
- **Grgic 2022 (acute fatigue MA), Sports Medicine**: числовых ES в abstract не приведено (вывод качественный: RTF → большее острое утомление). DOI: `10.1007/s40279-021-01602-x`

## Частота тренировок

- **Schoenfeld 2016, Sports Medicine** (MA гипертрофии, volume-equated):
  - «higher frequency being associated with a greater effect size than lower frequency (0.49 ± 0.08 vs. 0.30 ± 0.07, respectively)»
  - DOI: `10.1007/s40279-016-0543-8`
- **Grgic 2018, Sports Medicine** (MA силы):
  - «when the volume is equated, there was no significant effect of RT frequency on muscular strength gains» (числовых ES в abstract не приведено)
  - DOI: `10.1007/s40279-018-0872-x`
- **Hammarström 2018, Sports Med Open** (MA силы):
  - числовой итог: корреляция частоты и прироста силы приведена фрагментарно в извлечённом abstract («g 0.48 (95% CI 0.20–0.76)» — только во фрагменте); при volume-equated — «no significant effect of RT frequency on muscular strength gain»
  - DOI: `10.1186/s40798-018-0149-9`

## Периодизация

- **Grgic 2017, Sports Medicine** (MA, 18 исследований, 81 эффект, 1RM):
  - «the magnitude of improvement in 1RM following periodized resistance training was greater than non-periodized resistance training (ES = 0.43, 95% CI 0.27–0.58)»
  - DOI: `10.1007/s40279-017-0734-y`
- **Volume-equated periodization MA 2022, Sports Medicine** (35 исследований):
  - «muscle hypertrophy did not differ between LP and UP (ES 0.05, 95% CI [−0.20, 0.29]; Z = 0.36 (P = 0.72))»
  - «when volume is equated between conditions, periodized resistance training has a greater effect on 1RM strength compared to NP resistance training. Also, UP resulted in greater increases in 1RM compared to LP» — но только у тренированных
  - DOI: `10.1007/s40279-021-01636-1`
- **Rønnestad 2016, Scand J Med Sci Sports** (блок-периодизация, лыжники-элитники, 5 нед):
  - «BP achieved a larger relative increase in peak power output and power output at a blood lactate concentration of 4 mmol/L than TRAD (4 ± 4 vs −3 ± 6% and 11 ± 10 vs 2 ± 4%, respectively, both P < 0.01). BP also increased maximal oxygen uptake by 2 ± 2% (P < 0.05)»
  - DOI: `10.1111/sms.12418`

## Сила хвата и смертность

- **PURE 2015, The Lancet** (17 стран, n=139 691, медиана 4.0 лет):
  - «Grip strength was inversely associated with all-cause mortality (hazard ratio per 5 kg reduction in grip strength 1.16, 95% CI 1.13–1.20)»
  - DOI: `10.1016/s0140-6736(14)62000-6`
- **JAMDA 2017, J Am Med Dir Assoc** (MA проспективных когорт, community-dwelling):
  - «per-5-kg decrease in grip strength was 1.16 (1.12–1.20) for all-cause mortality, 1.21 (1.14–1.29) for cardiovascular diseases, 1.09 (1.05–1.14) for stroke, 1.07 (1.03–1.11) for coronary heart disease, and 1.01 (0.98–1.05) for cancer»
  - DOI: `10.1016/j.jamda.2017.03.011`
- **UK Biobank 2018, BMJ** (n=502 293, 13 322 смерти за 7.1 года):
  - конкретные HR per 5 kg в abstract приведены только как «were higher (all at P…)» — точные значения отсутствуют в извлечённом abstract; смотреть полный текст
  - DOI: `10.1136/bmj.k1651`

## VO2max / кардиореспираторная пригодность

- **Mayo Clin Proc 2022** (MA 37 когорт):
  - «the pooled RR for all-cause mortality comparing the top vs bottom tertiles of CRF levels was 0.55 (95% CI, 0.50 to 0.61)»
  - «For every 1-MET increase in CRF, the RR for all-cause mortality was 0.89 (95% CI, 0.86 to 0.92)»
  - DOI: `10.1016/j.mayocp.2022.02.029`
- **BJSM 2022 dose-response MA**:
  - «per 1 metabolic equivalent increase in CRF were 0.88 (95% CI 0.83 to 0.93), 0.87 (95% CI 0.83 to 0.91) and 0.93 (95% CI 0.91 to 0.96)» [all-cause, CVD, cancer]
  - «with highest CRF were 0.47 (95% CI 0.39 to 0.56), 0.49 (95% CI 0.42 to 0.56) and 0.57 (95% CI 0.46 to 0.70)» [all-cause, CVD, cancer; vs lowest CRF]
  - DOI: `10.1136/bjsports-2021-104876`
- **BJSM 2024 overview of SRs** (26 SR):
  - «CRF had the largest risk reduction for all-cause mortality when comparing high versus low CRF (HR=0.47; 95% CI 0.39 to 0.56)»
  - «every 1-metabolic equivalent of task (MET) higher level of CRF was associated with a 11%–17% reduction in all-cause mortality (HR=0.89; 95% CI 0.86 to 0.92, and HR=0.83; 95% CI 0.78 to 0.88)»
  - «incident heart failure… (HR=0.31; 95% CI 0.19 to 0.49)» high vs low; «every 1-MET higher level of CRF was associated with a 18% reduction in heart failure (HR=0.82; 95% CI 0.79 to 0.84)»
  - «cardiovascular mortality among those living with cardiovascular disease… (HR=0.27; 95% CI 0.16 to 0.48)» high vs low CRF
  - DOI: `10.1136/bjsports-2023-107849`
- **Ann Oncol 2015** (MA рак-смертность):
  - «intermediate and high levels of cardiorespiratory fitness were related to statistically significant decreased summary relative risks (RRs) of total cancer mortality of 0.80 [95% confidence interval (CI) 0.67–0.97] and 0.55 (95% CI 0.47–0.65), respectively»
  - DOI: `10.1093/annonc/mdu250`
- **Fitness vs fatness 2018, Prog Cardiovasc Dis** (MA CVD-смертность):
  - «Unfit individuals had two to three times the risk of mortality across all levels of BMI. Overweight and obese-fit individuals had 25% and 42% increased mortality risk, respectively, compared to normal weight-fit individuals.»
  - DOI: `10.1016/j.pcad.2018.07.004`

## Сон → тестостерон / метаболизм

- **Sleep Med 2021** (MA, 18 исследований, 252 мужчины):
  - «short-term partial sleep deprivation had no significant effect on male serum testosterone (SMD = −0.22; 95% CI: −0.5, 0.06; P = 0.13), while total sleep deprivation reduced the male testosterone levels (SMD = −0.64; 95% CI: −0.87, −0.42)»
  - DOI: `10.1016/j.sleep.2021.10.031`
- **Sleep Med Rev 2019** (MA 41 RCT, ограничение сна у здоровых):
  - «experimental sleep restriction resulted in a 0.34 kg weight gain (p = 0.003). Sleep restriction also decreased insulin sensitivity (standardized mean difference = −0.70, p < 0.01)»
  - «we did not find strong evidence supporting the significant impact of sleep restriction on mean leptin or ghrelin levels or energy expenditure»
  - DOI: `10.1016/j.smrv.2019.02.002`
- **Ann Am Thorac Soc 2015** (dose-risk MA, 18 исследований, 75 657):
  - «Those who report a sleep duration of less than 5 hours have a 1.5 higher odds of having metabolic syndrome» (референс 7–8 ч)
  - DOI: `10.1513/annalsats.201504-190oc`
- **Sleep Med Rev 2021** (36 cross-sectional + 9 longitudinal):
  - «OR = 1.14, 95% CI = 1.05–1.23» (короткий сон и prevalent MetS); «RR = 1.16, 95% CI = 0.95–1.41» (incident MetS, NS)
  - DOI: `10.1016/j.smrv.2021.101451`

## Анаболические стероиды — кардиориск

- **Baggish 2017, Circulation** (когорта 140 культуристов 34–54 лет):
  - «AAS users demonstrated higher coronary artery plaque volume than nonusers (median [interquartile range] 3 [0, 174] mm³ versus 0 [0, 69] mm³; P = 0.012)»
  - «increase… in rank of plaque volume for each 10-year increase in cumulative duration of AAS use: 0.60 SD units [0.16–1.03 SD units]; P = 0.008»
  - DOI: `10.1161/circulationaha.116.026945`
- **PLoS One 2016** (бывшие AAS-пользователи vs контроль):
  - «Former AAS abusers exhibited significantly lower median… total and free testosterone levels than control participants (total testosterone: 14.4 (11.9–17.7) nmol/l vs. 18.8 (16.6–22.0) nmol/l)»
  - DOI: `10.1371/journal.pone.0161208`
- **Rev Endocr Metab Disord 2021** (обзор): цифры в abstract не приведены — только качественные эффекты («AAS use markedly decreases high-density lipoprotein cholesterol (HDL-C) and increases low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C)»). DOI: `10.1007/s11154-020-09616-y`

## Автогрегуляция / RPE

- **Sports Med Open 2022** (MA автогрегуляции, 15 исследований):
  - «velocity loss thresholds > 25% demonstrated significantly greater hypertrophy compared to thresholds ≤ 20% (MD = 0.64, 95% CI 0.07 to 1.20 cm², p = 0.03, SMD = 0.34)»
  - «autoregulated and standardized load prescription produced similar improvements in strength»
  - DOI: `10.1186/s40798-021-00404-9`
- **Front Physiol 2018** (RPE vs %1RM, periodized, sets/reps matched):
  - «Bench press (1RMG +9.64 ± 5.36; RPEG + 10.70 ± 3.30 kg), squat (1RMG + 13.91 ± 5.89; RPEG + 17.05 ± 5.44 kg) and their combined-total 1RMs (1RMG + 23.55 ± 10.38; RPEG + 27.75 ± 7.94 kg) increased»; между группами значимых различий нет («p > 0.05»)
  - DOI: `10.3389/fphys.2018.00247`

## Velocity-based training

- **Dorrell 2020, J Strength Cond Res** (8-недельное RCT, VBT vs %1RM):
  - «maximal strength for back squat (VBT 9%, PBT 8%), bench press (VBT 8%, PBT 4%), strict overhead press (VBT 6%, PBT 6%), and deadlift (VBT 6%). Significant increases in CMJ were witnessed for the VBT group only (5%)»
  - «a significant interaction effect was witnessed between training groups for bench press (p = 0.004) and CMJ (p = 0.018)»
  - DOI: `10.1519/jsc.0000000000003089`
- **Sports Medicine 2023** (SR по velocity loss thresholds):
  - «The choice of VL threshold does not seem to affect strength and muscle endurance gains whereas higher VL thresholds are superior for enhancing hypertrophy, and lower VL thresholds are superior for jumping, sprinting, and velocity against submaximal loads performance» (конкретные эффекты — в мета-регрессиях, в abstract приведены только коэффициенты «b» без контекста)
  - DOI: `10.1007/s40279-022-01754-4`
- **IJERPH 2021** (SR, элитные атлеты, 7 исследований):
  - «applying velocity losses of 10–20% can help induce neuromuscular adaptations and reduce neuromuscular fatigue»
  - DOI: `10.3390/ijerph18105257`
- **Scand J Med Sci Sports 2020** (bench press, пороги VL):
  - «Higher VL thresholds allowed for a greater volume load which maximized muscle hypertrophy, whereas lower VL thresholds evoked positive neuromuscular-related adaptations. No significant differences were found between groups for strength gains»
  - DOI: `10.1111/sms.13775`

## Где данных мало (пусто значит пусто)

- **Robinson 2024 (RIR dose-response)**: точные коэффициенты dose-response в abstract не приведены — только качественный вывод. Смотреть полный текст (`10.1007/s40279-024-02069-2`).
- **UK Biobank BMJ 2018**: HR per 5 kg не раскрыты в abstract.
- **Grgic 2018 (frequency strength MA)**: ES в abstract не приведены.
- **Блочная периодизация в силовых видах**: мета-анализов по силовым характеристикам мало в 2015–2026 (доминируют нарративы — Stone 2021, Issurin 2016 — и вынослостные RCT).
- **Сон→тестостерон**: единственный мета-анализ — 18 исследований / 252 мужчины (Sleep Med 2021); ключевые классические работы (Leproult & Van Cauter 2011 и др.) старше 2015 и не входят в окно поиска.
